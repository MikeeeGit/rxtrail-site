RxTrail privacy policy

Updated 28 October 2026 · Covers RxTrail and RxTrail Demo

Study records stay on your device unless you choose to export them. No account, advertising or analytics is required. This policy explains the separate handling of exports, support emails and website visits.

Who provides RxTrail

RxTrail and RxTrail Demo are provided by Stonepath Digital Ltd, company number 17478686, registered office Unit A, 82 James Carter Road, Mildenhall, IP28 7DE, United Kingdom. They are offline study companions for adult prescribing learners. They are not patient-record systems. Do not enter patient names, identifiers, clinical records or other confidential information.

This policy covers the Android app, its optional export and import features, and the support and policy website. The app requires no account, contains no advertisements or analytics SDK, and has no Internet permission. Stonepath Digital Ltd does not receive your study records automatically and does not operate a server that stores or synchronises them.

Information kept on your device

The app saves practice sessions, question and option order, draft and submitted answers, selected units, results, whether help was used, repeat-attempt status, study timestamps and review schedules. It also saves bookmarks, topics marked read, portfolio preparation status, non-identifying evidence references, course-date changes and their reasons, and practice-activity dates, minutes, categories and supervision categories.

Your preferences include study route, session length, appearance, Pip and animation choices, reminder settings and whether setup is complete. Local record IDs identify study records, not an advertising account. Session timing includes the device's restart count to detect when a saved timer can no longer be trusted. Calculator entries and screen state can remain locally while you use or resume a screen.

These records support the features you choose, including resuming sessions, showing progress, scheduling review and organising study. They are kept in the app's private Android storage. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, health records or advertising ID.

Exports and imports are your choice

Export progress creates an unencrypted JSON file containing saved answers and sessions, progress, preferences and planning entries. It does not include the teaching bank. The file may contain any text you entered into planning fields, so use non-identifying references only. It is not password-protected by RxTrail.

You choose the destination using Android's file picker. If you choose a cloud document provider, that provider may upload and store the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Stonepath Digital Ltd does not automatically receive the export. Anyone you give the file to, or who can access its location, may be able to read it.

Import reads only the progress file you select and shows a preview before merging it into local records. The app does not scan all your files or request broad storage access. Exported files remain outside the app's private storage and can remain after the app is removed.

Optional reminders and permissions

Reminders are scheduled locally at the time you choose. On Android versions that require it, notification permission is requested when you enable reminders. You can decline it and continue using the study features. You can turn reminders off in RxTrail Settings or Android's notification settings.

Reminder notifications use generic study text and do not include your answers, scores or planning entries. Android controls how notifications appear, including on the lock screen. The app receives restart, clock-change and timezone-change events to reapply local scheduling. It does not use push messaging or an exact-alarm permission.

External reference and support links

Reference, support and public-policy links open another app, normally your browser, only when you choose them. RxTrail passes the selected public link and does not attach your study progress. The browser and destination website can process normal connection information, such as an IP address, and apply their own privacy policies.

The app's policy text is bundled and can be read offline. You do not have to open a website to read it or use the study features.

Storage, backup and security

RxTrail relies on Android's app-private storage and your device's security protections. It does not add a separate app PIN or its own encryption to the local database or exported JSON files. Protect your device and choose export destinations carefully.

The app is configured to disable automatic Android backup and to exclude its private records from Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Device migration tools are controlled by Android and the device manufacturer and can behave differently, so do not rely on automatic backup to preserve your progress. Use the explicit export feature when you want a recoverable copy.

The app includes standard Android, AndroidX, Kotlin and JSON-processing libraries for its interface and local functions. It does not include a third-party advertising, analytics or automatic crash-upload service. Your operating system or app store may process installation or diagnostic information under its own settings and policies.

Keeping and deleting your records

Local study records have no automatic expiry and remain until you remove the app's data. To delete them, open Android Settings, find RxTrail or RxTrail Demo under Apps, and use Storage > Clear storage or Clear data. Wording varies by device. Uninstalling without retaining app data also removes the app's local records. This resets your saved progress and preferences; Stonepath Digital Ltd cannot recover records that you have not exported.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete exported files, copies shared with other people, or copies kept by a document provider. Delete those separately in the destination app or service, including its trash or backup copies where applicable. Stonepath Digital Ltd cannot remotely view or delete records held only on your device or in a destination you selected.

Support correspondence

For privacy questions or support, email support@tellyforge.com and include RxTrail in the subject. This is Stonepath Digital Ltd's support contact.

If you choose to email us, we and our email service receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. Stonepath Digital Ltd is the controller for personal information in that correspondence. We use it to respond to requested support and investigate app issues, relying on our legitimate interests in providing support and maintaining app security. Where applicable, we also process information to meet our legal obligations.

RxTrail does not automatically attach an export or diagnostic report. Do not send patient information; avoid sending your full progress file unless it is needed and you have removed any confidential information.

Support correspondence is separate from local app records. How long we keep it depends on what is needed to resolve the request and any applicable legal requirements. You can contact us to request deletion.

Your privacy rights and complaints

You can ask for access to or correction of personal information we hold, and request deletion or restriction where applicable. Other rights, including portability, apply where the law provides them.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

For a privacy request or complaint, contact support@tellyforge.com with the subject RxTrail privacy so we can try to resolve it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

These rights concern information we hold. We cannot remotely access or delete study records held only on your device or exports in a destination you selected. Follow the local deletion and export instructions above for those records.

The public policy and support website

The RxTrail policy and support website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when a visitor is not signed in. Its privacy statement explains its handling of website request information. This is separate from using the offline Android app. The RxTrail website does not receive your local study database or an automatic progress export.

GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The date above identifies this version. Updated app releases include their policy text; the public policy page shows the published policy. A change to the website alone does not silently add network features to an installed app.
